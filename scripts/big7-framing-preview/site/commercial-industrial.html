Before we get started

A few good
questions.

Can we contact Big 7 specifically for framing or drywall?

Yes. Commercial wood framing and drywall are the main specialties. Include the scope, project location and the crew support you need in your estimate request.

Can Big 7 help with other trades?

Big 7 has crews for additional work. Availability and the appropriate scope need to be discussed for each project; the website does not promise every trade for every job.

Do you promise a completion date online?

No. Scope, site conditions, coordination and timing are discussed for the individual project. Quality comes before shortcuts.

What should I include in an estimate request?

Start with your contact information, project location, type of work and a short description. Mention whether plans or other project information are available.
