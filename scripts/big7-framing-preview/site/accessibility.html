Using this website

Accessibility.

We want people to be able to explore Big 7’s services and contact the company without unnecessary barriers.

Navigation and reading

The site includes a skip-to-content link, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, descriptive form labels and controls for light and dark themes. Page content remains available without JavaScript.

Motion and display

The design respects your device’s reduced-motion preference. Text and layouts are designed to adapt to smaller screens and browser zoom. Some features may depend on your browser and assistive technology.

Images

Illustrative imagery is labelled as AI-generated and is not represented as photography of Big 7’s completed projects.

Need help?

If something on the site prevents you from accessing information, email info@big7construction.com. Include the page and a description of the difficulty. Do not send sensitive information.

Preview status

This is a design candidate. Its forms do not send inquiries. Testing is documented with the implementation; this statement is not a blanket certification of accessibility compliance.
