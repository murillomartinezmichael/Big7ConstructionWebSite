## What we're actually asking for, and why

Four testimonials came off the site on 2026-08-05. They were written as draft
copy during the build — matched to real projects, but never sourced from the
customers whose words they claim to be. The consent behind them was recorded on
Big 7's behalf rather than collected from the people quoted, which isn't consent.

So this is not "may we put your quote back up." It's "here's a line we drafted
about your project — is that fair, would you say it in your own words, and may
we publish it with your name?" That's an easier ask than it sounds, and it's the
only version that's true.

Only the owner can make it. He has the relationships; we don't.

## What counts as consent, and what we do with it

**Sufficient:** any written reply — email, text, or a signed line — from the
customer that (a) approves specific wording and (b) says whether their name can
be used. A forwarded "Use it" is enough. Verbal approval relayed second-hand is
not.

**Where it lives:** the reply gets saved (screenshot or forwarded email) and a
one-line receipt goes in `DECISIONS.md` — customer, date, quote, name-or-no-name
— before the quote goes near a page. No receipt, no republish.

**If there's no reply:** don't chase, don't infer. The quote goes back only if
the owner can confirm the customer actually said something to that effect, and
then it goes back unnamed and unspecific enough that nobody can be identified
from it. Note in `DECISIONS.md` that it's owner-confirmed rather than
customer-confirmed, so the weaker sourcing is visible later.

**If neither:** it stays off. The site was audited green without these and reads
fine; the case study still carries the project's real numbers.
